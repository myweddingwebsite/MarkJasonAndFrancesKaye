How It Began

Our Love Story

"What started as a random chat became God's perfect plan."

August 4, 2023

A Random Hello

People say love finds you when you least expect it, and that's exactly how our story began. One ordinary day, I decided to try my luck on Omegle. I wasn't expecting anything serious, just another random conversation with a stranger.

Then, I met him. From our very first conversation, something felt different. My first thought was, "Lahi siya sa uban." Instead of asking the usual questions, he was more concerned about me. He reminded me not to drink too much and encouraged me to go home early. Such simple words, yet they carried genuine care.

For the first time, I met someone who made me feel safe even through a screen. We exchanged Instagram accounts and continued talking there.

Days Later

The Question That Changed Everything

I've always been the type of person who values clarity. "Sigurista na, dili na ganahan og duwa-duwa." I've experienced enough to know that I didn't want a relationship without purpose.

So, just one day after we chatted, I asked him something most people would probably wait months to ask: "Are you interested in me? Because I'm not looking for a relationship just for fun. I'm looking for someone to settle down with." Without hesitation, he answered, "We have the same goal."

That simple answer gave me peace. There were no mixed signals, no mind games — just two people being honest about what they were praying for. Looking back now, I realize that our love story began with courage and honesty.

The Weeks After

Love in the Little Things

The days that followed were filled with conversations that seemed endless. We talked about everything — our dreams, our fears, our families, our faith. And slowly, two strangers became each other's favorite person.

His actions always spoke louder than his words. He would surprise me by sending food and snacks to my workplace, not because he had to, but because he wanted me to know that he was thinking about me. Those simple gestures reminded me that love isn't always found in grand romantic moments.

August 11, 2023

Our First Date

Finally, the day came when we met in person. Our first date was at Starbucks Ebloc 2, IT Park. To be honest, I was terrified — it was my first time meeting someone I had only known online, and I didn't know what to expect. But the moment we saw each other, everything felt easy.

We laughed. We shared stories. We talked about life. We exchanged endless chika and, of course, a little chismis. Hours passed without us even noticing. When I went home, my heart quietly whispered, "Lord... basin siya na gyud ni."

September 3, 2023

Our First "Yes"

The first Sunday of September became one of the most meaningful days of our lives. Together, we attended Holy Mass at the Basilica Minore del Santo Niño. In the middle of the Mass, I quietly asked him, "What are you praying for?" He smiled and shared his prayer, then asked me the same question.

I looked at him and said, "I'm praying that Sto. Niño will continue to guide us as we begin this new chapter of our lives, especially now that we're making our relationship official." It wasn't just the beginning of our relationship — it was the beginning of a relationship built on faith. From that day on, Santo Niño became more than just a church to us. It became a sacred place in our love story, a place where we entrusted our relationship to God.

October 28–29, 2023

Becoming a Family

Falling in love wasn't only about the two of us — it was also about the two little hearts that mean the world to me. As a mother, introducing someone to my children wasn't something I took lightly; it was one of the biggest decisions I could ever make.

On October 28, I finally introduced him to my two beautiful children, Kiara and Jhaz. At first, they were shy and curious, wondering if he was simply Mama's friend or workmate. But he never forced himself into their hearts. Instead, he patiently spent time with them — laughed with them, played with them, took care of them. And through those little moments, he showed them the same love he had been showing me all along.

The next day, we introduced Kiara and Jhaz to his family. Watching them welcome my children with genuine love filled my heart with overwhelming joy. At that moment, I realized something beautiful: God wasn't just writing a love story between two people. He was creating a family.

August 26, 2026

The Second "Yes"

By then, we had already talked countless times about spending the rest of our lives together. We were already planning our future. But he still wanted to give me the proposal every woman dreams of, and he knew exactly where it had to happen.

He brought me back to the Basilica Minore del Santo Niño — the very place where I first said "Yes" to becoming his girlfriend. Standing before the same God who had been with us from the beginning, he asked me another life-changing question. This time, he asked me to become his wife. And once again, with tears in my eyes and love in my heart, I said, "Yes."

It was a beautiful full-circle moment. The same place that witnessed the beginning of our relationship now witnessed our promise of forever. Santo Niño has been with us from our first "yes" to our forever "yes." For us, it has always been more than a church — it has been a constant reminder that when we put God at the center of our relationship, He faithfully writes every chapter better than we ever could.

And Now

Forever

Looking back, it's amazing how one random conversation on Omegle became the greatest blessing of our lives. What seemed like coincidence was actually God's perfect timing. He knew exactly when our paths should cross. He knew exactly what our hearts needed. He knew that our story wouldn't just be about finding love — it would be about building a home, creating a family, growing in faith, choosing each other every single day.

Today, as we stand before God and everyone we love, we do so with hearts overflowing with gratitude. To our family and friends, thank you for being part of our journey. To Kiara and Jhaz, thank you for filling our lives with love and laughter — you have always been, and always will be, one of God's greatest blessings to us. And to each other: thank you for choosing love, thank you for choosing patience, thank you for choosing forever.

Our story began with one unexpected message. Today, by God's grace, it becomes our happily ever after.

"Therefore what God has joined together, let no one separate."

— Mark 10:9
